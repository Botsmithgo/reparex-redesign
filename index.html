Good questions

The honest answers

Is Reparex a dye?

No. Dye coats your hair in pigment that grows out as obvious roots. Reparex isn't a dye — it helps your hair restore its own natural color from within, so the change is gradual and there are no roots to give it away.

How fast will I see results?

Most people notice a difference in about 5 days, with natural color typically restored within 1–2 weeks of regular use. Individual results may vary.

Will it look fake or orange?

Because it restores your natural shade rather than depositing a one-size colour, the result blends in — no flat block of color, no brassy tone. That's exactly why no one can tell.

Is it safe? Any harsh chemicals?

Reparex is free from the harsh chemicals found in many dyes. As with any cosmetic product, follow the instructions and patch-test if you have sensitive skin. If you're pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor first.

Will it stain my skin or pillowcase?

Used as directed, Reparex is designed to work cleanly — no gloves required, and no messy stains on skin, sinks, or pillowcases the way many dyes and rivals leave behind.

What if it doesn't work for me?

You're covered by our 60-day money-back guarantee. Try it, and if you're not happy, we'll make it right. After 30+ years, we're confident you'll love the results.
